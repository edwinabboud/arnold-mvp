# Privacy Policy — Arnold Coach

**Effective date:** June 10, 2026
**Last updated:** June 10, 2026

Arnold Coach ("Arnold," "the app," "we") is an AI-powered calisthenics coaching app operated by Edwin Blanco, an individual developer based in Spain ("the developer"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what your rights are.

We keep this simple on purpose: Arnold collects the minimum data needed to coach you, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone for advertising.

## 1. What we collect

**Account data.** Your email address and a password, used to create and secure your account. Authentication is handled by Supabase, our backend provider.

**Training data.** The information you provide and generate while using the app: your selected program path, training schedule, experience level, strength benchmarks, body weight, completed sessions, sets, reps, weights, rest times, session duration, skipped exercises, and reported pain or discomfort (body area and severity). This is the data Arnold uses to build and adapt your training plan.

**Chat messages.** Messages you send to Arnold's coaching chat, together with relevant training context, are processed to generate coaching responses (see Section 3).

**Analytics data.** We collect pseudonymous usage analytics (e.g., which screens and features are used, onboarding completion, session and chat activity) through PostHog, hosted in the European Union. Analytics are linked to a random account identifier, never to your email address. We do not use analytics for advertising and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

**What we do NOT collect:** your name (unless you put it in a chat message), location, contacts, photos, health records from other apps (no HealthKit integration), payment details (the app currently has no purchases), or advertising identifiers.

## 2. Why we collect it (legal bases under GDPR)

- **To provide the service** (contract performance): account data and training data are required for Arnold to generate and adapt your training plan.
- **To improve the product** (legitimate interest): pseudonymous analytics help us understand what works and fix what doesn't.
- **To keep you safe and informed** (legitimate interest / legal obligation): we record your acknowledgement of the in-app health and AI disclaimer.

## 3. AI processing

Arnold's coaching chat is powered by Anthropic's Claude API. When you use the chat, your message and relevant training context (such as your current plan, recent sessions, and strength estimates) are sent to Anthropic's API through our secure server to generate a response. We send the minimum context needed for good coaching. Per our configuration, this data is used to generate responses to you — it is not used to train Anthropic's models under the API terms applicable to our integration.

Arnold's coaching is generated by software, including AI. It is not a medical professional and its guidance is not medical advice.

## 4. Who processes your data (sub-processors)

| Provider | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Authentication, database, secure API proxy | EU-hosted project |
| Anthropic | AI chat responses (Claude API) | United States |
| PostHog | Pseudonymous product analytics | European Union |
| Apple / Expo (EAS) | App distribution and builds | US/EU |

Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (e.g., to Anthropic in the United States), transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as applicable to the provider.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Ever.

## 5. How long we keep it

Your account and training data are kept while your account exists. If you delete your account, your data is deleted from our production systems (see Section 6). Pseudonymous analytics events are retained per PostHog's standard retention and are not traceable to you after account deletion. Backups expire on a rolling basis.

## 6. Your rights and controls

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, object to the processing of, and delete your personal data.

- **Delete your account** directly in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and associated training data from our systems.
- **Export your data** or exercise any other right: email us at the address below and we'll respond within 30 days.
- **Complain** to a supervisory authority. In Spain, that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD, www.aepd.es).

## 7. Children

Arnold is not directed at children. You must be at least 16 years old to use the app. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (TLS). API keys and credentials are stored server-side only and never shipped in the app. Database access is restricted per-user with row-level security.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update this page and the effective date, and notify you in the app where appropriate.
